Two-semester capstone project charter
CP1 + CP2 · complete engineering system
PROBLEM TO SOLVE
Many cities have roadside cameras but no traffic counts, because turning video into reliable vehicle counts means
handling oblique camera angles, occlusion in dense traffic, mixed vehicle types and weather that degrades the
image. The consequence is an unreliable visual result that can fail silently under realistic scene and sensor
conditions.
WHO BENEFITS AND WHAT OWNERSHIP IS ESTABLISHED
Primary beneficiaries are system operators and reliability engineers; the cited source establishes context or
source custody but does not identify an operational problem owner. No sponsor, funder or supporting organisation
for the KL student implementation is stated. The KL deployment stakeholder and operational decision owner must
be confirmed before approval.
FINAL ACHIEVEMENT REQUIRED
Deliver roadside video traffic counting and congestion forecasting validated under occlusion, rain and night
conditions.
REFERENCE TO REPRODUCE IN CP1
Handle mixed vehicle types and oblique perspective. This is the O2 reference against which every Traffic Volume
Estimation and Short-Term Congestion Forecasting from Roadside Video contribution must be compared.
DIFFERENTIATING CONTRIBUTION IN CP2
Sustain accuracy under occlusion, rain and low light. This is the O3 technical contribution that must be
implemented, isolated and evaluated separately.
COMPLETE FINAL DEMONSTRATION
Forecast near-term congestion from estimated volumes. The final demonstration must show the complete Traffic
Volume Estimation and Short-Term Congestion Forecasting from Roadside Video workflow rather than an isolated
component.
INDEPENDENT ACCEPTANCE
Validate counting and forecasting against reference counts across conditions. The result must be independently
checked under representative, boundary and failure conditions.
WHY THIS IS CAPSTONE-WORTHY
This is a two-semester culminating project because the team must establish the O2 reference “Handle mixed
vehicle types and oblique perspective”, design and evaluate the O3 contribution “Accuracy under occlusion, rain
and low light”, integrate the O4 system “Forecast near-term congestion from estimated volumes”, and defend the
O5 result under independent, boundary and failure conditions.

O1
Validate the problem and freeze the engineering contract
Build vehicle detection and counting from roadside video under a fixed camera geometry. Verify the beneficiary,
source claim, system boundary, evidence access, interfaces, hazards and acceptance rules before implementation
proceeds.

requirements, architecture decision record, risk register and capture and annotation checks, geometry consistency,
scene slices and confidence evidence.
O2
Build the reproducible reference and vertical slice
Handle mixed vehicle types and oblique perspective. Compare at least two feasible reference approaches, select
one through a documented trade-off and measure it under AC-1 and AC-4.
CP1 Gate 2 — a reproducible reference and an integrated vertical slice are demonstrated.
Mandatory outcome and evidence
Perspective- and class-robust counting. Deliver executable fixtures, an independent oracle, unit tests, resource
profile, failure log and baseline values for counting accuracy under adverse conditions; congestion forecast error;
cross-camera generalisation; critical-scene failure rate (%).
SEMESTER VIII / CAPSTONE PROJECT II
O3
Engineer and prove the differentiating contribution
Sustain accuracy under occlusion, rain and low light. Compare it with O2 using the same evidence and resource
envelope; include ablation, sensitivity, trade-off or controlled experiments appropriate to the project.
CP2 Gate 3 — the contribution is technically defensible and its trade-offs are measured.
Mandatory outcome and evidence
Counting accuracy under adverse conditions. Report baseline-versus-candidate results, uncertainty, condition-wise
failures and a defensible positive or negative conclusion. Retain the selected configuration, experimental controls,
raw evidence and a trace from each claim to its acceptance result.
