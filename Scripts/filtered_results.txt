Accessible Feature-Revealing Enhancement and Visualisation of Earth-Observation Imagery
Project mandate: The project team shall design, engineer, optimise, validate and deploy Accessible
Feature-Revealing Enhancement and Visualisation of Earth-Observation Imagery as an integrated,
professional-quality system. Treat the cited industry problem as the governing requirement; establish
stakeholder needs and measurable acceptance criteria before selecting the architecture or
implementation approach.\n\nProblem context and prescribed solution direction: NASA imagery
holds features — faint structures, subtle changes, fine detail — that are hard to perceive at ordinary
scale or resolution, and much of it is inaccessible to people who can't easily interpret raw scientific
images. The consequence is an unreliable visual result that can fail silently under realistic scene and
sensor conditions.
